Before you join.

The questions most teams ask first.

How quickly will I receive work?

Most requests are delivered in two to three working days. Larger projects are split into stages that each fit the delivery window, so you see progress every few days rather than at the end.

Can I add multiple requests?

Yes. Add as many as you like. Work moves through the queue one request at a time, in the order you set, and you can reorder whenever priorities change.

Who does the work?

A senior designer, the same one over time. There is no junior layer and no account manager between you and the work.

What do I keep when the membership ends?

Everything we made for you: the finished designs and the source files, in the tools you already use.

Can I pause the membership?

Yes, any time, from your dashboard. The days you haven’t used wait for you until you come back.
