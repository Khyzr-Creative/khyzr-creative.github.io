What this website collects

Nothing, by itself. This site is a handful of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, has no forms and no accounts, and loads no third-party scripts. What moves on the home page is drawn in your browser, and nothing about how you use it is sent anywhere.

One thing still happens when you open a page, as it does on any website: our hosting provider records the request in its server logs, including your IP address, the page you asked for, the time, and which browser you used. That is how the page reaches you and how the service is kept running. We do not use those logs to build a profile of you.

The typefaces and pictures are served with the page from our own host, so opening a page sends nothing to anyone else.
